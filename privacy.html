Privacy policy

A starter privacy notice for Bloomberg Dev. This draft must be reviewed and adapted to your actual business, systems and legal obligations before publication.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Bloomberg Dev (“we”, “us” or “our”) is associated with the website bloomberg-dev.com. We aim to handle personal information fairly, securely and transparently. This draft is not legal advice and does not confirm the final data practices of your business.

1. Who is responsible?

Business contact details supplied for this website: Bloomberg Dev, 729 Green Lane, Dagenham, England, RM8 1UT; email: info@bloomberg-dev.com. Before launch, confirm the legal entity name and the appropriate privacy contact.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features enabled, information may include your name, email address, telephone number, property address or postcode, property preferences and any details you choose to include in a message. Technical information such as browser type, device data and website usage may also be processed if analytics or similar tools are enabled.

3. How information may be used

  • To respond to property enquiries and requests.
  • To discuss property buying, selling or valuation services you have requested.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To meet applicable legal obligations and handle legitimate business records.

Only use each purpose where it reflects your actual practices and where an appropriate lawful basis applies.

4. Lawful bases and your rights

Under UK data protection law, the lawful basis may depend on the purpose and circumstances, and can include steps taken at your request before a contract, contract performance, legal obligations, legitimate interests or consent. You may have rights to request access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection or portability, subject to legal conditions. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

5. Sharing and service providers

Personal information may be shared with service providers that support hosting, email, enquiry handling, security or analytics, where applicable. Confirm the providers you use, their locations, contractual safeguards and any international transfers before publishing this policy. Do not sell or disclose personal data except as lawfully permitted and accurately described.

6. Retention and security

Information should be kept only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose, legal obligations and legitimate business needs. Appropriate technical and organisational security measures should be used, although no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Cookies and embedded content

This starter site uses browser storage to remember a cookie-choice prompt. If you add analytics, advertising, maps, video, chat or other third-party services, update this policy and your cookie controls to accurately explain what they do. Non-essential cookies and similar technologies should only be used in line with applicable requirements.

8. Children

This website is intended for people seeking property information and is not designed to collect children’s personal information knowingly.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when our practices or legal requirements change. For privacy questions, contact info@bloomberg-dev.com.

Pre-launch action: verify this policy against your real data flows, complete your lawful-basis assessment, set retention periods, identify processors, and seek UK privacy advice where needed. Replace any statement that does not match your business.
