Cookie policy

This starter policy explains the current demo preference control and what to review before enabling additional technologies.

Last updated: 29 October 2026

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored by a website in your browser. Similar technologies can store or access information on your device.

What this demo uses

The demo site stores your choice about the cookie notice in browser local storage. This helps the notice stay dismissed after you make a choice. It is not currently connected to an analytics or advertising platform.

Optional cookies and third-party tools

If the live website uses analytics, advertising, embedded maps, video, chat or other third-party tools, update this policy to list those tools, their purposes, the information they collect, retention and relevant providers. Configure consent controls before any non-essential cookies or similar technologies are set, where required by UK law.

Managing your choices

You can manage cookies and site storage through your browser settings. Clearing site data may cause this website to show the cookie notice again. A production consent tool should let visitors make and later change choices in a clear way.

Contact

For questions about cookies, contact info@bloomberg-dev.com.

Before launch, audit all scripts, embeds and hosting services, then update this policy and consent mechanism to reflect the actual website. This starter text is not legal advice.
